# Proposed Figure Style

Domain: machine unlearning / ML; audience: WWW research reviewers.
Goal: show measured recall-utility tradeoffs, not manufacture GSU optimality.
Profile recommendation: ai-computing; white background, compact sans-serif,
colorblind-safe colors plus marker shapes, separate model panels, English text.
Venue: WWW draft context; no claim of verified official figure-format compliance.
Intake: research topic, audience and figure goal supplied in thread; proposed
English/accessible manuscript style still awaiting user approval. No formal
figure has been rendered. Export after approval: matching PDF, 300dpi PNG, SVG.

Evidence invariants: separate architecture/model/environment/input strata;
different marker fill for F0-only versus F0+F1; Original is no-training control.
Plot all valid methods, including dominated GSU and weak baselines. No jitter of
data coordinates, interpolated smoothed curves, invented uncertainty or stars.
Use points for endpoints; only connect prespecified ordered sweep settings.
Single-seed finite-set nondominance is descriptive, not mathematical/global
optimality or a statistically established frontier.
